Technology Overview

Continuous Inspection via Public Transport

Unlike conventional surveys requiring dedicated LiDAR/inspection trucks costing lakhs of rupees, DRISHTI AI mounts low-cost Android phones behind the windshields of municipal transit buses. As buses complete daily passenger routes, our lightweight vision engine scans road conditions with zero dedicated driver effort.

Dual Sensor Fusion (Camera + Accelerometer)

Camera vision alone can mistake dark shadows, bitumen patches, and painted road lines for potholes. DRISHTI fuses optical detection across 3 continuous frames with the device's Z-axis IMU vertical accelerometer shock (≥ 1.25g). This eliminates 85% of false detections.

Multi-Bus Spatial Deduplication

When 5 buses pass the same pothole on Station Road, DRISHTI clusters sightings within 18m and road heading into a single Master Incident ID. Instead of spamming the PWD with 5 tickets, the municipal officer receives 1 verified ticket backed by 5 sighting timestamps.
