The Innovation: Multi-Fleet Passive Inspection

Rather than commissioning dedicated inspection cars or waiting for citizen grievance backlogs, DRISHTI AI deploys rugged edge cameras across existing public transport buses, municipal waste trucks, utility vans, and police patrols. As vehicles traverse their fixed daily runs, they inspect road corridors multiple times daily.
