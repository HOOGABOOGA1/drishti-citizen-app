The Core Innovation: Zero-Capex Urban Sensing

Traditional municipal monitoring requires costly survey teams or waits for citizens to complain. DRISHTI AI places compact edge AI cameras inside existing public transport buses and municipal sanitation trucks. As vehicles traverse their normal daily routes, they continuously inspect every meter of roadway multiple times a day.

STEP 1
Vehicle Camera
Ruggedized HD optical camera mounted behind bus windshield.
STEP 2
Edge AI Detection
Lightweight YOLO-based model detects potholes, streetlights, and hazards.
STEP 3
GPS + Sensor Fusion
Sub-meter GPS synced with vertical chassis shock acceleration (IMU).
STEP 4
Verification Consensus
Multi-frame tracking eliminates shadow and puddling artifacts.
STEP 5
Duplicate Clustering
Incidents within 15 meters merge into a single persistent Incident ID.
STEP 6
Ward/Dept Dispatch
Automated ticketing to PWD, Electrical, Sanitation, or Jal Board.
STEP 7
Field Repair
Municipal work crew fixes hazard and submits completion photo.
STEP 8
Optical Verification
Subsequent bus pass captures repair photo; Before/After verified.
