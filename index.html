AI Bus Vision Technology

Automated road defect detection using moving public transit

The Core Problem & DRISHTI Solution

Cities possess hundreds of public transport buses traveling every major corridor daily. By mounting standard edge camera nodes with on-device computer vision, the city receives continuous, automated road inspections without relying solely on manual complaints.

1. On-Device Edge Inference

Neural detection runs locally on the bus unit. Zero cloud video bandwidth cost. Only verified defect crops and telemetry are transmitted.

2. Multi-Frame Consensus

A defect must be tracked across 3+ consecutive video frames to prevent false alerts caused by optical glare, shadows, or debris.

3. Camera + IMU Sensor Fusion

When a pothole is visually detected, onboard 3-axis accelerometer readings correlate the vertical shock peak to estimate physical depth.

4. Spatial Deduplication

When multiple buses or citizens pass the same crater, sightings are merged under one permanent Incident ID with independent sighting counters.
